# Architectural Decision Log

Record significant architectural decisions here as the project evolves. No architectural decisions have been recorded yet; the planned architecture is a direction, not a finalized design.

Use sequential identifiers such as `ADR-001`. Keep entries focused on the problem, reasonable alternatives, the chosen approach, and its consequences. If a decision changes, preserve the original entry and link to the decision that supersedes it.

## Entry template

```markdown
## ADR-XXX: Decision title

- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Status: Proposed / Accepted / Superseded

### Context
What problem or constraint requires this decision?

### Options considered
What reasonable approaches were considered, and what are their tradeoffs?

### Decision
What approach was chosen, and why?

### Consequences
What benefits, limitations, and follow-up work result?

### Related decisions
Link to related or superseding entries, if applicable.
```
